### What changed in 0.2.42

- Sync recovers on its own now: file watchers rewriting files mid-sync are retried, the index is refreshed before giving up on a stash, and the origin is verified before anything is preserved or moved.
- Repos with an unfinished merge, cherry-pick, revert or rebase are left alone with their reason instead of failing, and resume automatically once finished.
- Deleted or unreachable origins skip the repo without touching local work - no more stash churn on gone remotes.
- Two projects sharing one folder name each get their own checkout (`name-2`) instead of blocking every sync.
- Switching the Quick Chat profile or device closes the previous conversation and opens the new one instead of stranding the panel.
- Terminal notification cards show the session and device in the title row, with only the message in the body.
- The collapsed toolbar menu is fully opaque in transparency mode, and tile Auto sizing uses a layout icon.
