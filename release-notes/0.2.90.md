### What changed in 0.2.90

This release brings a Worktrees dialog for every session, resizing that ends where you let go, smooth tile dragging with a live preview, files you drop opening straight away, and a cleaner look across the app.

#### Worktrees and checkpoints
- Worktrees is now its own dialog, opened from a session's top bar. It lists the worktrees of the session's repository, or of every repository directly inside a folder of projects, with search, All / Not merged / Merged filters, Open PR, Merge and Remove.
- Setup for new worktrees opens as a dropdown under each repository's Setup button. Examples in its fields read as examples, not values.
- Checkpoints moved to the top bar too, beside Worktrees. The top bar's buttons sit in groups split by dividers, which stay even when you hide a button.

#### Tiles
- Resizing ends exactly where the outline showed, and dragging an edge to the bottom of the window fills to the bottom. Tiles beside a taller one stack instead of copying its height.
- A resize that you retry right away is no longer taken for a double-click, and the first click on PzzaCode while another app is in front already resizes, drags or presses.
- Dragging a tile is much smoother: a small live copy of the whole tile, with what its terminal shows right now, follows the pointer.

#### Files
- Files and folders dropped onto PzzaCode open straight away as file and folder tiles.
- A file you drop onto PzzaCode or open from Finder opens and saves even outside your home folder. Only files you open this way are allowed; key and credential folders stay protected.

#### Agents
- New setting in Settings > General > Terminal: Auto-trust folders. Off by default. When on, Claude Code's "trust this folder" question is answered for you; tool approvals still ask.

#### Look and feel
- Notifications show the session and device, with the device's logo, in a smaller line under the title.
- Lists in Agent sessions, Worktrees and Checkpoints no longer show text through their headers while scrolling in semi-transparent mode.
- Dashed lines are now thin solid lines, the device badge in a session's top bar is tighter, the Refresh sessions reminder lost its dot, and several icons, step numbers and small labels line up and read better.
