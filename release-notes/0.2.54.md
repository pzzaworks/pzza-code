### What changed in 0.2.54

- The macOS traffic lights are now centered in the top bar with equal top, left and bottom insets and the same gap to the brand. The position is measured on-device instead of guessed, and the brand inset collapses in fullscreen where the lights leave the window.
- The first-run tour tooltip no longer teleports on open: the centered popup keeps its offset through the enter animation, the anchor measurement is confirmed on the next frame, and keyboard focus never scrolls the page.
- OpenCode usage cards now show a masked API key fingerprint (first and last characters only, the secret never leaves the backend), so multiple keys across devices are easy to tell apart. Cards with different fingerprints are kept side by side.
