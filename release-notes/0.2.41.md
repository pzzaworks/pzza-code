### What changed in 0.2.41

- The usage panel no longer shows a bare "usage 401" card for Codex accounts without a usable entitlement. Rejected or expired Codex credentials hide the card instead.
- Claude accounts with expired or rejected session tokens are hidden from the usage panel rather than showing an error row. Running the Claude CLI refreshes the token and the card returns on the next scan.
- The new-session name field no longer draws a chunky inner outline on focus. The outer bar shows the same subtle accent glow as the other inputs.
