### What changed in 0.2.77

This release fixes a crash when stopping dictation, makes dictated text arrive sooner and replaces the flickering word preview with a calm status, moves terminals onto a new version by themselves after an update, and lets seven more agents pick up their conversations after Refresh sessions.

#### Dictation
- Pressing Stop no longer crashes PzzaCode with microphones that record at 48 kHz, the rate of most built-in and USB microphones.
- The caret now shows a soft "Listening" while you speak and "Transcribing" while a phrase is written, instead of words that kept changing as they were recognized. Each phrase appears once, when it is typed.
- Text is typed sooner after you pause: about half a second instead of close to a second.
- The first word of a new phrase is no longer typed twice, and no longer typed as a guess at a word you had barely started.

#### Terminals after an update
- The first launch of a new version now refreshes terminals that kept running onto it, so they get PzzaCode's permissions (Full Disk Access, Automation, Screen Recording) again without a manual Refresh sessions. Agents continue their conversations.

#### Agents continue after Refresh sessions
- GitHub Copilot CLI, CodeBuddy Code, Letta Code and Qoder CLI continue the last conversation in their folder.
- Cline, goose and ForgeCode continue the exact conversation they had open, found in their own session stores.
