### What changed in 0.2.76

This release rebuilds live dictation so it keeps up with how people actually talk, switching between languages, pausing to think and speaking softly, and fixes terminals losing their permissions after an update.

#### Dictation, rebuilt
- Speech is now recognized phrase by phrase: each pause ends a phrase, which is read in the language it was spoken in and typed once. Words already typed never change again, and only the phrase you are speaking moves in the preview.
- Switching languages mid-dictation no longer turns a sentence into a translation of itself ("then open a pull request" typed as Turkish, or a Turkish sentence typed in English).
- A softer phrase after a louder one, from leaning back or turning away from the microphone, is no longer taken for silence and dropped.
- The first word after a pause is no longer swallowed, a word is no longer typed twice where a long sentence was split, and a short phrase such as "Okay, thank you." is no longer padded with a made-up word.
- Words still being recognized are shown dimmed at the caret, so a correction no longer looks like typed text being deleted, and dictating right after a word adds the space before it.

#### Permissions after an update
- After an update, terminals that kept running stay on the previous version until Refresh sessions, and until then macOS lets none of PzzaCode's permissions reach them, even though System Settings shows them allowed (Full Disk Access, Automation such as controlling other apps with `osascript`, Screen Recording). PzzaCode now says so after launch and points to Refresh sessions.

#### Refresh sessions
- Refresh sessions no longer fails with "no current target" while restoring sessions, which could leave a tile showing "[exited]" although its session was back.
