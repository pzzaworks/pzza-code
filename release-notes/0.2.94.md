### What changed in 0.2.94

This release gives the folder picker favorites and recent folders, splits Settings → Setup into a Security section with a page per group, keeps the Port manager's filters on one line, and fixes the workspace tab that lost its top edge while a session was dragged onto it.

#### Folder picker
- Star the folder you are in to keep it as a favorite. Favorites and your recent session folders sit in one compact row of chips under the path, per device; a click opens the folder.
- The row scrolls sideways when it is full, with the edges fading instead of a scrollbar. Remove a favorite with the small x on its chip.

#### Settings
- Setup is now Security, with its own pages like General: Tools, Permissions, tmux permissions, Default editor and This device. Each page shows its state and its own actions on top: Allow all and Refresh sessions on the permission pages, Run setup on Tools.
- Clicking an open section again (General, Security, Connections and the others) folds its pages away.

#### Port manager
- The filter chips stay on one line and scroll sideways, fading at the edges instead of wrapping or showing a scrollbar.
- Group headers (each session and Other services) sit on a rounded band with the count in a pill, like repository headers in Worktrees.

#### Workspace tabs
- Dragging a session onto another workspace tab no longer cuts off that tab's top edge, and the active tab joins the grid below it.
- The dragged session's preview stops at the top bar instead of sliding under it.
