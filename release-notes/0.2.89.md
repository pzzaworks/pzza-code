### What changed in 0.2.89

This release turns the update dialog for busy terminals into a quiet reminder.

#### After an update
- When something still runs in your terminals, Refresh sessions waits beside the version in the top bar instead of a dialog. The terminals move to the new version on their own once everything is idle, and the reminder goes away.
- Clicking it asks first and names the sessions where something is running, the same as Refresh sessions in Settings > Setup.
