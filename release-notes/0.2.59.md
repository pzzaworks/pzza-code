### What changed in 0.2.59

- Fullscreen and workspace transitions now fit instantly instead of holding a frozen-small frame: the redraw is covered by a blur veil tinted from the terminal's own background (matching opaque and semi-transparent modes), fading on the first parsed bytes with a bounded fallback so it can never stick.
- The fullscreen animation no longer zooms the tile, so terminal text never stretches mid-transition.
