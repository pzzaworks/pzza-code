### What changed in 0.2.55

- Agent usage cards are now calmer: the spend details (estimate note, token trend, daily breakdown, Today, Yesterday and Last 30 days) sit collapsed behind a "More" expander instead of always taking up space. Nothing is removed, it just opens on demand per card.
- The "More" row is full-width clickable while the label stays left aligned, so tapping anywhere on the row - including the empty right side - toggles it.
