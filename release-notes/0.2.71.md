### What changed in 0.2.71

#### Updates
- When an update needs you, a button appears beside the version in the top bar: Update (with automatic updates off), the download progress, then Restart once the new version is installed. Hover it for the full message.

#### Setup and tour
- Setup is one centered column with the logo above the welcome title, without step icons or animations between steps.
- In semi-transparent mode, setup darkens the desktop about as much as a session tile and blurs it more strongly, instead of showing it bare. The Back and Continue buttons no longer sit on a solid block.
- The UI tour always follows setup, also when setup runs again from Settings > Setup.
- In semi-transparent mode, the tour's cards are liquid glass like dialogs and menus.

#### Menus and settings
- Every menu and select list opens with the same short fade. Top bar dropdowns and the workspace panels no longer animate their height while their content loads.
- Select lists are as wide as their longest option and never wrap it, and move left instead of leaving the window. "Claude Code" and "OpenCode" in the new session form stay on one line.
- The OpenCode mark follows the text color like the other agent marks.
- Every Settings page starts at the same distance from its edges. Saved prompts keeps its add form apart from the list, with even spacing in rows and fields.
